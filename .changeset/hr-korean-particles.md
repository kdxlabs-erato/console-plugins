A Korean sentence that carries a name writes its particle in the one form the name takes, as the console's own Korean does beside it: "휴가 종류 sick을 저장했습니다", "규정 kr-fiscal-year를 저장했습니다", "메신저를 통해 신청", where both forms stood, "을(를)". A sentence that took a name as its topic says it another way: "김민수가 2026-12-31에 퇴직합니다", "병가에는 사유를 적어야 합니다".
