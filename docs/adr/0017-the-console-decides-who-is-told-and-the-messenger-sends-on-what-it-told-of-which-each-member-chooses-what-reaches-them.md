# ADR-0017: The console decides who is told, and the messenger sends on what it told, of which each member chooses what reaches them

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-25
- Supersedes points 1 and 2 of [ADR-0015](0015-what-a-person-is-told-of-their-own-work-is-theirs-to-choose-on-the-console-and-through-their-agent.md), under which the messenger read who a change is about and kept what each chose to be told of. Amends [ADR-0008](0008-every-notification-leaves-the-console-through-the-messenger-plugin-which-reads-what-it-tells-as-a-principal.md): a notification still leaves the console through the messenger alone, and who is told of the board is no longer its to decide.

## Context

Two things told a member of their own work by two sets of rules. The console woke an agent account for what asks something of it. The messenger told a person, and an agent account that has an account in Slack, by rules of its own: it read a task's owner and workers, a decision's deciders and an agent account's manager, and kept what each chose to be told of. An agent account was told twice, a task could be turned on or off in neither place, and what one is told of the console's work was set in a plugin.

The console decides it from 0.6.1 on (its ADR-0157). It makes a notification for each member an event concerns, under a kind the member has on, keeps the list under a bell, lets a member turn a kind, a project and one task on or off, and wakes an agent account by the same rules. With the capability `notifications:read` it hands a plugin, with each event, whom it told: `data.told`, each `{ personId, kind, woken, followed }`. That list is the same for every plugin and the most a plugin sends on; which of it a plugin sends is the plugin's own setting, kept and offered by the plugin.

## Decision

1. **The messenger sends on what the console told, to whom it told it, and no more.** It holds `notifications:read` and reads `data.told` of each board event it hears: a direct message to each member named there, under the kind they were told. It reads no owner, worker, decider or manager to find whom to tell, and tells nobody the list does not name. Whoever made the change, a project turned off, a task's bell and whether one's own agents count are the console's rules, already in the list.
2. **Whom the console woke is sent nothing.** An entry that is `woken` is an agent account the console told itself, by a wake: the messenger sends it to nobody, so that an account is told once.
3. **A member chooses which of it reaches them in Slack, in the messenger.** Each of the console's kinds, by the console's names, is sent on or kept out of Slack; so are the tasks whose bell they turned on themselves (`followed`), as a choice of their own, since such a task tells them of everything on it whatever their kinds; and the messages come at once or in one message a day. Whoever never chose is sent everything the console tells them, as it happens. A kind that is off in the console makes no notification and reaches Slack by no choice made here; a choice made here never tells a member of more than the console does.
4. **The choice is kept and offered by the messenger alone**: in its own records, by the member and the kind; on a page of its own, which leads to the console's settings for what one is told at all; and by the command `notify` and the query `notices`, an agent account choosing as a person does. The console's settings name no plugin, and the messenger writes none of them.
5. **How it is carried stays the messenger's.** The words in the member's language, the workspace it is sent in, the button that turns off what let a message through, the day's message, and when a thing is worth a message at all: an agent account the console says is unheard, or stopped by its usage limit, each time its state changes, is sent on once it lasted and once it is back, to whom the console told of it when it began and when it ended.
6. **The records move to data version 4.** A member's record keeps what is not sent on, and nothing of who is told. Of a record of before, which kept every kind on or off, the kinds that were on unless turned off and that the member had off stay off; the rest follows the console. The projects a member turned off and whether their own agents count are not carried over: a plugin cannot write a member's settings in the console, where they are chosen from now on.

## Consequences

- **A person who chose nothing in the console is sent their agent accounts alone**, which is the console's default, where the messenger sent a task given, a move, a comment and a decision to whoever never chose. A member who wants those in Slack turns the kind on in the console first; the release says so, as a breaking change.
- **An agent account is sent nothing of the board in Slack**: every notification of an account is its wake.
- **A member has two places to choose in, each of one thing**: the console's settings, of what they are told at all, and the messenger's page, of what reaches Slack. The page and the skill say which is which.
- **Two kinds are new to Slack**, a task ready to begin and a decision of one's own answered, and a decision an agent account raised for its manager reads as a decision where the console tells it under their agent accounts.
- **The messenger asks one capability more**, consented to by an administrator before the version runs, and runs on consoles from 0.6.1. It still reads the boards (`board:read`), for a task's name and for the plugin that raised a decision, and the directory, for the account an agent's event names.
- **Rejected:** keeping the messenger's own rules beside the console's, which tells an agent account twice and keeps one setting in two places; sending a followed task by the kinds chosen here, which would send part of a task the member asked to be told everything of; sending it whatever is chosen here, which leaves a member no way to keep it out of Slack; carrying a member's projects turned off into the console, which no call of a plugin's may write; a capability the messenger may go without, which would leave a page that chooses nothing where an administrator did not turn it on.
