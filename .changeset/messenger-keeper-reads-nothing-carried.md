Breaking: whoever keeps the messenger reads how it runs and nothing of what it carries. The role `messenger` could read the plugin's records through the console (`console plugin messenger records`), and there whom a message was sent on to and which task or decision it was of, those of projects they may not see among them, and who asked for leave, who decides it and how it ended, which HR shows to them and to HR alone.

- **The records are an administrator's**, all but a member's own choice and what waits for their day's message, which stay theirs. The pages and the commands read none of them and show what they did: `status`, `people`, `routes`, `channels` and `activity` answer whoever keeps the messenger.
- **`activity` no longer answers `thing`,** the id of the task, the decision or the request a line was of, and lists no event that was for nobody, which most lines were. A task's or a decision's event that was for nobody leaves no line at all. A line keeps whom a message reached only while one that failed part way is tried again.
- **The Activity page says what it left unsaid:** how many a notification was sent on to, that nobody the console told takes it in Slack, and a decision answered by its name. A leave event nobody of was in Slack to be told reads as passed over, and no longer as sent.

A line kept by an earlier version is forgotten 30 days after it was written.
