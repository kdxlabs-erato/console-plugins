Each page is titled as its entry in the menu names it: the page that connects Slack is Connection, and a project's tab is Messenger; the sentence beside the title still says what it does with Slack. A warning on a page is a few words in bold and the sentence under them, where the whole sentence was bold, and it stands at the top of the page's main column on every page, on Routes too, where it ran across the form beside the list. The Activity page names the console's own events as it names HR's (task created, decision raised, and the rest) and keeps the raw name only of an event it has no words for. A moment is written as the console writes one, the month in short, the day and the time, with the year only where it is not this one. In Korean the pages call a task by one word throughout, the console's own.
