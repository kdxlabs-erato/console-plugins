Built against console 0.5.3, and it runs on the consoles it ran on before. What 0.5.3 changes is the console's own: a job runs every period it declares, so that the messenger's day's message goes within five minutes of its time where a console before sent it up to six after; and the front page of someone who has arranged nothing shows each widget at the size it starts at, as HR's are declared already (Announcements large, My leave and Who is out medium, Approvals small). HR's editor of announcements takes the latest fixes of the library it is built on.
